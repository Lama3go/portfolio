Who Is Behind The Screens

Al-Madinah → Vancouver

Good design does what a good host does. It notices what you need before you have to ask for it.

I trained as a graphic designer, then spent two years in travel and hospitality: reservations, visas, guest relations, and a very busy airport floor. I learned what confusion looks like on a person's face in real time, which is a thing you cannot learn from a usability report.

Now I design mobile interfaces. I care about the small moments where an app is either calm or cruel: the empty state, the error message, the moment you are late and looking for your gate. I work in Arabic and English, and I design for both directions of the page.
